## 一、正文块(EN,可直接入稿)

### The retention rule makes "too hard" indistinguishable from "already solved"

Our surgical arm removes exactly one clause from the scaffold gate, leaving every other repair path intact. Its proximal effect is complete — 100% of its trained mob levels sit on floor 2, with zero floor rewrites — yet its trained mob levels are replayed 1.0× on average, receive the lowest grade in 29 of 30 cases, and never re-enter the active set. The distal effect, however, is at full strength: conditional hit rates of 0.80 (warrior) and 0.60 (bat), all four frozen thresholds passed. Something removes these levels from the curriculum while the skill they teach transfers.

The cause is not the gate but the retention rule the gate feeds into. New levels are trained once in the session of their birth, scored, and then must win a place in a fixed-capacity active set by a compare-and-swap against the lowest-scoring incumbent; the score is the standard learnability quantity `sr·(1−sr)` over the task's own success rate. This function is symmetric and vanishes at both ends of its domain. **A level the agent cannot touch (sr ≈ 0) and a level the agent has fully mastered (sr ≈ 1) receive the same score, and are therefore evicted by the same rule.**

The gate's remaining repairs strip premarks and inventory grants that are not themselves violations — median premark count falls to 0 for the surgical arm, against 1 for the gate-off arm and 3 for the no-stack baseline; median task-level success rate falls correspondingly to 0.0, against 0.6 and 0.1. With its subsidies removed, a floor-2 combat level is unsolvable at birth, scores zero learnability, ties with every mastered level in the pool, and is dropped after a single exposure. The arm's entire floor-2 curriculum therefore consists of 30 single-exposure levels, against 201 levels replayed 3.0× in the gate-off arm.

We call this the **loop tax**: the cost of a validation repair is not paid in the quality of the level it edits, but in the level's eligibility for replay. It is invisible to any audit of the edited artifact — the code is correct, the floor is correct, the target achievement is correct — and it is measurable only in the retention statistics.

Two consequences are worth separating. First, the tax delays rather than blocks: the surgical arm's distal cluster responds in full, and its return trajectory is still rising at the 2e9 truncation, so a starved curriculum is not a failed one at this budget. Second, and more general: **any curriculum system that retains levels by a learnability-style criterion inherits this blind spot**, because such criteria are by construction indifferent between "not yet learnable" and "no longer learnable." A repair, filter, or difficulty adjustment that pushes a level's success rate toward zero does not merely make it hard — it makes it, to the scheduler, look solved.

This is the mirror image of the dependency result in §5. There, scaffolding *granted* to a level raises its task-level success rate and lowers its episode length while degrading the curriculum. Here, scaffolding *removed* from a level drives its task-level success rate to zero and evicts it from the curriculum. In both directions the task-level success rate is a poor proxy for curricular value, and in both directions the system optimizes the proxy.

## 二、中文批注(供组内与讲稿使用)

- **一句话版**:门卫修复剥掉补贴 → 任务级 SR 掉到 0 → learnability = sr(1−sr) = 0 → **在保留擂台上与"已掌握"任务同分,必被淘汰** → 只剩出生那一次曝光。
- **为什么这条重要**:它把回路税从"我们观察到重放少"升级成"评分函数的结构性盲点",而且**不依赖 Craftax**——任何用 learnability 类判据做保留的系统都继承这个盲点。这是全篇泛化性最好的机制之一。
- **和 §5 的关系**:§5 是"给脚手架 → 任务级指标变好、课程变坏";本块是"撤脚手架 → 任务级指标归零、关卡被淘汰"。**同一枚硬币的两面:任务级 SR 在两个方向上都不是课程价值的好代理,而系统两个方向都在优化这个代理。**
- **代码出处**:`src/dicode/evolution_efficient.py` 的 `attempt_to_activate_task`(池满时对最低分活跃任务做 compare-and-swap)+ `conf/dicode_manager/default.yaml` 的 `score_function: "learnability"`、`active_task_capacity: 100`。该行为继承自上游,非本工作引入。

## 四、诚实条款(须随本块一起入稿)

1. **单臂**:surgical 为 n=1;认证种子在飞行中。保留统计的**方向**在三臂间一致(0/1/3 与 1.0×/3.0×/1.8× 同序),但量级不作跨臂显著性声明。
2. **api-ref**:surgical 与 base15 的 prompt 含 api-ref,gateOff 不含;该通道已单独测量并给出上界(kwarg 错误减半,但被供给过剩吸收)。保留统计与 prompt 内容无因果路径,但跨轮定量对照仍带此 caveat。
3. **泛化为假说**:"任何 learnability 类保留判据都继承该盲点"是**机制推论**,在本工作中仅有一个系统的证据;表述用 "inherits this blind spot **by construction**",不写 "we show that all such systems…"。
4. **不写"门卫有害"**:surgical 的远端全额响应,且总分与 gateOff 无可测差异(−0.20 / +1.14)。正确表述是"修复的代价落在保留资格上,而非关卡质量上"。
